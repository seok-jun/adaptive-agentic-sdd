# SDD plan-change

## Plan correction and policy transition

If a premise of the plan turns out to be wrong during implementation or verification, or if a project policy to be applied to in-progress work changes, correct the current instructions by the following path. Small and below use the Issue's plan and records and do not create an SDD or a separate transition document. Do not keep a wrong instruction because the PLAN was reviewed, and do not hide the difference only in the SUMMARY.

An AS-IS correction in the new artifact layout includes every affected source among the human-facing status and risks in `README.md` and the direct evidence in `01-as-is.md`. Do not fix only the evidence and leave the human-facing judgment premises in their past state.

1. Identify the evidence of the finding and the affected premises, Decisions, contracts and verification, and stop only the edits and runs that depend on that premise. Needed fact investigation and independent work may continue. Writing temporary tests outside the existing allowed boundaries is not automatically permitted either.
2. If a settled decision, product meaning, scope or authority changes, apply the existing human-judgment rules. Decisions already made or a valid delegation may be used as the basis, and no new approval is created for a simple factual correction. The implementer does not arbitrarily choose an alternative outside the delegation.
3. Correct the wrong instructions in the current Issue and the relevant AS-IS/TO-BE/change-plan, and leave in the existing records the previous revision, the replaced sentences/items, the reason for the change, the evidence and the decision reference. Preserve the original revision as history. If the original text cannot be corrected immediately, state the replaced target and the new instruction in a linked correction document/record, and make it discoverable from the entry point of the current plan. If that link cannot be created because of authority or concurrency problems, do not resume the affected work. Do not make the implementer guess which of two instructions is current.
4. A policy transition records the Issue, phase and time to which it applies and the existing policy it replaces. Check the active owners, reviews in progress, uncommitted changes and recent work records, and coordinate the application interval. Earlier runs and reviews are preserved as records of the policy and target at that time. Distinguish a simple document relocation from a change in meaning, and do not automatically reset work already done.
5. Apply the self/required review of the current grade and [re-review by meaning and impact](../design-review.md#design-re-review-by-meaning-and-impact). Distinguish the evidence invalidated among the existing reviews and verification from the evidence that can be kept. Do not repeat the whole A/B merely because there is a new SHA or a policy change, and do not shrink an important change in meaning into a document correction. If a verification input changes, update that verification according to [PASS validity](../verification.md#verification-sequencing-and-pass-validity).
6. After securing the needed current-target evidence, align the references and applied policy of the Issue, plan and Execution Packet, hand over to the relevant owner and resume. At closing, confirm that the corrected plan, the actual implementation and the verification agree.
